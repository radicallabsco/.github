# Conduit

### Automate the boring parts of architecture and engineering.

**[Conduit](https://getconduit.us)** is an AI automation platform for architecture and engineering teams.

We help teams find what's already been solved, automate what hasn't, and keep their tools useful as projects change.

## What we're building

A place for teams to discover, build, deploy, and manage the tools that make their work easier.

- **Discover** solutions that already exist within your firm.
- **Reuse or adapt** a colleague's tool to fit your workflow.
- **Build what's missing**, from Revit add-ins to cloud applications, by describing what you need.
- **Deploy and share** tools so the rest of your team can use them.
- **Keep improving** those tools as your projects and processes evolve.

## Why Conduit

Architecture and engineering teams solve practical problems every day. Too often, those solutions stay on one person's machine, serve only one project, or disappear when the person who built them moves on.

We're building Conduit to help that work become something the whole firm can find, use, and improve.

**Solve it once. Keep it useful and discoverable for your team.**
